Case Study

Airline Commercial Analytics Dashboard

Interactive Power BI dashboard for analyzing airline revenue, markets, routes, sales channels, and ticket issuance activity through business-oriented KPIs and visual insights.

Challenge

Commercial data was difficult to interpret across markets and routes

The commercial team needed a clear and interactive view of sales performance across markets, routes, points of sale, and ticket issuance activity. The available data required transformation and business-oriented analysis before it could support decision-making.

Solution

A business-focused Power BI dashboard for commercial analysis

The project transformed raw airline commercial data into an interactive Power BI dashboard with KPIs, geographic analysis, route-level insights, and filters for exploring sales activity from different business perspectives.

Key Features

Outcome

Business Insights

The dashboard provided a centralized view of commercial activity, making it easier to compare markets, routes, and sales channels and to identify relevant geographic and operational patterns in the data.
